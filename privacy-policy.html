Ma3ak Kam Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Ma3ak Kam is a personal finance application designed to help you record accounts, income, expenses, installments, associations, and financial plans. The application is designed to work locally on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app can store the financial records, account details, settings, attachments, and backup files that you choose to enter. These records are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded by Ma3ak Kam to a developer-operated server.

SMS and notification access

If you enable the related Android permissions, the app may read financial SMS messages or selected financial notifications on your device to suggest transactions. Processing happens locally. Non-financial messages are not imported into the app database, and the app does not send SMS messages.

Advertising

Ma3ak Kam may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads. Google and its partners may process device, diagnostic, and advertising information according to their policies and your Android/Google advertising settings. During development, test ads are used.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy.

Backups and sharing

If you create or share a backup, the file is handled by the method and destination you choose. Backup files may contain sensitive financial information. Do not share them with people you do not trust.

Data deletion

You can delete records, attachments, backups, or the app from your device. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored data according to Android device behavior. Data copied to a destination outside the app must be deleted from that destination separately.

Children

Ma3ak Kam is a personal finance tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality changes. The latest version will be published at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact address shown on the Ma3ak Kam Google Play listing.
